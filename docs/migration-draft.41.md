## English

The undelivered draft.40 TS24 candidate retains its exact sources and failures.
Draft.41 corrects a contradiction: with one prior expected destination, any
qualified foreign target produces NC even if the expected target is added to
the same source. Ambiguity cannot erase that independent failure. Unqualified
annotations or divergent hypotheses stay unknown without an independent failure;
identical repetitions remain idempotent.

Only TS24-A01 methodology changes. The xdefault-role-v1 input format is unchanged;
normative/interpreter versions distinguish results. Never overwrite a draft.40
result or silently reuse its qualification. Use retained originals in a distinct
dossier with exact tools and actually supplied review; preserve earlier attempts.
No human review or authority is fabricated. The two bound TS23 native readers
are unchanged; complete engine identity remains recorded separately.

Raw inconclusive conformance observations are not reports. Contradictory provenance
is refused by the public report CLI before dossier creation, exit64. A minor NC
remains NC when decision policy gives GO_WITH_RESERVATIONS; no human authorization.

Full engine tests, exact archive reconstruction and explicit app import/reception
are required separately. Frozen demo, earlier imports, archives and tags stay
unchanged. No publication, deployment or implicit0.2 transition.
